# Cost to Run the Blaster Service Business, by State

**Owner:** negotiator | **Contributors:** negotiator (state research), matt-claude (Telnyx fee table, verified live)
**Status:** draft — state fees verified against state filing agencies 2026-10-04. Telnyx fees verified against Telnyx's own pages 2026-10-04.
**Scope:** the cost of *operating* one managed-SMS services entity in a given state — the entity, its compliance, and the 10DLC registration it must do on behalf of its first tenants. Not a price list for customers.

---

## The six states, and why these six

matt asked for PA, Chicago/IL, GA, TX, plus two of my choosing. I picked **Florida** and **Ohio**.

| State | Why picked |
|---|---|
| **Florida** | No individual state income tax. Formation is $125 and the annual report is the most punitive in the set ($400 late fee). Dense auto-body/tinting/detailing market — Miami-Dade and Broward are among the largest collision and auto-services markets in the country, so it is the best proof market for our target niche. |
| **Ohio** | No annual report at all and no recurring state fee, so the ongoing cost of the entity is the registered agent and nothing else. Large Rust Belt auto-services base (Cleveland, Columbus, Toledo) plus heavy tinting culture. Best ongoing-cost structure of any large state. |

Between them they give the contrast matt-claude will want: FL is high-compliance/low-tax, OH is low-compliance/low-cost, and PA/IL are low-compliance/moderate-tax.

### The honest read on this table

**The entity is not the cost.** Across all six states the recurring state cost is $0–$139 a year. Anyone who frames this as a $300/mo business because Texas charges $300 to form has the wrong end of it — the $300 is one-time, and it buys a franchise-tax obligation forever.

**The real cost is the 10DLC registration per tenant**, which is where the money actually is. That is Part B.

**One trap worth naming:** Texas and Florida look free on state tax and are the two most expensive states to get *dissolved* by inattention. Florida's late fee is $400 — 57× Pennsylvania's entire annual compliance cost — for a filing that takes five minutes on Sunbiz. Set the calendar entry or pay an operator to do it.

## Part B — The 10DLC registration cost per tenant (the actual cost of doing this business)

These are **Telnyx's own published figures**, pulled from Telnyx's pages on 2026-10-04 and verified line-by-line. Telnyx states it passes these through without markup.

| Fee line | Who charges | Amount | Per |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand registration, EIN brand | TCR, passed through | $4.50 | once |
| Brand registration, sole proprietor | TCR | $4.00 | once |
| **Standard vetting** | TCR vetting partner | **$41.50** | per request |
| Enhanced vetting (optional, Aegis) | per request | $101.50 | per request |
| **Campaign review** | carriers, passed through | **$15.00** | **per submission** |
| Campaign fee, standard use cases | TCR | $10.00 | per month |
| Campaign fee, low-volume mixed | TCR | $1.50 | per month |
| Campaign fee, sole proprietor | TCR | $2.00 | per month |
| Carrier fee, outbound SMS part | AT&T / T-Mobile / Verizon | $0.0035 / $0.0045 / $0.005 | per part |
| Telnyx local number with SMS | Telnyx | $1.10 | per month |
| Telnyx US outbound SMS | Telnyx | $0.004 | per part |

**First payment, per properly vetted tenant:** $4.50 brand + $41.50 vetting + $15 first campaign review + $30 (three months of the $10 campaign fee, collected up front) + $1.10 number = **~$92**, before any message volume.

**Telnyx's own worked example**, which is the number to put in front of a prospect because it is Telnyx's arithmetic and not ours: first payment $65.60–$68.60 including brand, campaign review, three months of campaign fee, one number and 2,000 messages; then $16.10–$19.10/mo for months two and three; and $26.10–$29.10/mo from month four.

### The three facts that decide the pricing

**1. Vetting is $41.50 and it is the only line item that buys capacity.** It sets the daily throughput cap. Telnyx's published tier table:

| Vetting score | AT&T (per campaign) | T-Mobile (per brand, per day) |
|---|---|---|
| 75–100 | 4,500 msg/min | 200,000 |
| 50–74 | 2,400 msg/min | 40,000 |
| 25–49 | — | 10,000 |
| 1–24 or unvetted | 240 msg/min | 2,000 |

Telnyx's own line: *"At T-Mobile's default of 2,000 messages a day, a 50,000-message send takes 25 days to finish."*

$41.50 moves one brand from 2,000 messages a day to 200,000 — a 100× jump. Ten unvetted brands reach 20,000/day combined and cost nothing extra in vetting fees; one vetted brand reaches 200,000 for $41.50. **Multi-brand-for-capacity loses to single-brand-by an order of magnitude.** The only reason to run many brands is tenant isolation for real customers, never throughput.

**2. Campaign review is $15 per submission, not per campaign.** Rejections get resubmitted and paid for again. Onboarding pricing must assume 2–3 submissions. Two rejections on one campaign costs $45 in review fees before a single message sends.

**3. A brand holds up to five campaigns on Telnyx**, and each campaign holds multiple numbers. Five numbers, one campaign, one brand is well inside the limit.

### The cost model that matters: state × tenant

| | PA | IL | GA | TX | FL | OH |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entity year-one (DIY) | $132 | $225 | $150 | $300 | $263.75 | $99 |
| Entity steady-state | $7 | $75 | $50 | $0 | $138.75 | $0 |
| Per-tenant first payment (vetted) | ~$92 | ~$92 | ~$92 | ~$92 | ~$92 | ~$92 |
| **10-tenant setup cost** | **~$1,052** | **~$1,145** | **~$1,070** | **~$1,220** | **~$1,184** | **~$1,019** |
| **Steady-state, 10 tenants** | **$150** | **$1,003** | **$1,103** | **$260** | **$1,499** | **$260** |

Ten tenants' steady-state cost is dominated by the 10DLC campaign fees and message volume — roughly $10/tenant/month in campaign fees — plus per-part carrier fees. The entity is noise next to it. Florida is the only state where the entity's own annual filing ($138.75) is worth more than ten tenants' campaign fees.

| **Platform fee, 5,000–25,000 msg/mo tenant** | **$500** | |

Message cost passes through at cost or at Telnyx's rate with no markup. The platform fee is for the control plane: tenant isolation, consent gate, inbox, suppression, reporting, and the operator's time on a registration that can be rejected.

**The honest limit on this table.** It earns money on the tenants' own contacts. It does not add sending capacity for anyone's existing leads, and it does not make a cold outbound blast compliant. Telnyx is explicit that registration does **not** disable spam filtering — registered messages with spammy content, public link shorteners or all-caps urgency still get filtered. Do not let a prospect think the $41.50 makes unsolicited texting safe.
